# ♿ Accessibility & Usability

> **Tag**: #a11y #accessibility
> **Related Notes**: [[Design System]]

- **Touch Targets**: Minimum 44x44px touch targets on mobile buttons.
- **ARIA Attributes**: Buttons feature descriptive `aria-label` attributes.
- **Focus Rings**: Non-intrusive focus management for keyboard navigation.
- **High Contrast**: Dark theme exceeds WCAG AA contrast standards for primary text.
