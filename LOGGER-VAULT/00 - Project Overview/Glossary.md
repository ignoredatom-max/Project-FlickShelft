# 📚 Glossary — Terms, Entities & Concepts

> **Tag**: #glossary #reference
> **Related Notes**: [[Data Models]], [[Ratings and Reviews]], [[Media Logging]]

- **Diary Entry (`DATA`)**: A single record of a consumed movie, TV series, or video game with date, status, rating, and notes.
- **Watchlist (`WL`)**: A queue of upcoming or unreleased media items the user intends to watch or play.
- **Top Picks**: Four showcase media items chosen by the user and prominently featured on their profile.
- **Rating Tiers**: Emotive 5-stage rating: *Skip, Mediocre, Decent, Excellent, Peak*.
- **Rewatch Detection**: Automatic tagging of a media entry as `rewatched: true` when logging a title previously completed.
- **Public UID (`PUBLIC_UID`)**: A Firebase UID passed in the `?u=` URL parameter that triggers read-only public profile mode.
- **Movie Wall**: The full-screen edge-to-edge poster collection showcase that replaced the experimental canvas graph view.
